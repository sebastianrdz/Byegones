## How availability was checked

WebSearch (`site:apps.apple.com "<name>"` and variants) in this session, date 2026-11-21. This is a **screening, not proof**: the search index is incomplete and US-only. The App Store category is extremely crowded with swipe photo cleaners (dozens of "Swipe/Sweep/Tidy/Flick + Photo Cleaner" titles), so a distinctive name matters more than a descriptive one. **Definitive checks the user must do:** reserve the name in App Store Connect (it rejects taken names), and search USPTO TESS and EUIPO for class 9 and 42 before launch. None of this is legal advice.

Names I screened and **rejected** because an exact-name photo cleaner already exists: TidyRoll (swipe cleaner, iOS 17+, paid Pro tier), SnapSweep (at least 5 photo-cleaner listings), SwipeTidy (photo cleaner), Keepsy (Android swipe cleaner of the same name), Whisk (also a well-known Google image tool, so trademark and search noise).

## Candidates

### 1. Rollaway - "Swipe away photo clutter"
- **Rationale:** "Camera roll" plus "roll away / throw away". Two syllables, easy to say and spell, playful, and the subtitle carries the function. "Swipe", "away" and "photo" are in the subtitle, which helps search without cluttering the name.
- **EN / ES:** EN: reads as "roll away" (camera roll, getting rid of stuff). ES: stays "Rollaway" (not translated); works as a brand word, and "roll" echoes "carrete" (camera roll). Needs a native-speaker gut check. Spanish subtitle carries the meaning ("Limpia tu galería deslizando").
- **Availability:** No exact "Rollaway" app found in the search. Near names: Rollyssey (free endless-roller game, tagline "Roll away!"), Rollga (foam-roller companion), Roll Mobility, Clear Roll (puzzle game, iPad). None are photo tools, none is a near-identical title.
- **Trademark concerns:** "Rollaway" is also a generic term for a folding rollaway bed, and there are likely bed and furniture marks using it (class 20, unrelated to software). Low risk for class 9 apps but run the clearance search. No brand names in the name.

### 2. Shoo - "Swipe junk photos away"
- **Rationale:** What you say to clutter: "shoo!". Tiny, funny, memorable, fits the quick-game tone, and a one-syllable name looks great on the home screen.
- **EN / ES:** EN: instantly understood. ES: "shu" is understood as the same shooing sound ("¡shu!"), though the spelling "Shoo" is English; slightly less obvious but still fun.
- **Availability:** No app named "Shoo" found. The search only returned shoe-shopping apps and Shooyou (pet record app), Shoobs (events). Very short names are more likely to be taken in less-indexed storefronts or by dormant apps, so confirm by reserving.
- **Trademark concerns:** Short common word; possible marks in other classes. Low-to-moderate, needs a real clearance search. Weak SEO on its own (name carries no keyword), the subtitle compensates.

### 3. Lightly - "Swipe. Clear. Breathe."
- **Rationale:** Cleaning up should feel light, not heavy; also "travel lightly". Calm, premium tone, the opposite of the aggressive "cleaner" apps. Name works as a promise: lighter phone, lighter mind.
- **EN / ES:** EN: natural adverb, friendly. ES: not translated, "Lightly" reads as an English brand word; the "light" idea ("ligero", "liviano") is easy to explain in the subtitle. Less playful than #1/#2.
- **Availability:** No "Lightly" app found in search for the photo/storage space (query also covered "Lighten Up" and "Lightenup"). The name is a common English word, so an unrelated app named Lightly in another category is plausible; the search did not surface one, but it is not conclusive.
- **Trademark concerns:** Common dictionary word, probably used as a mark in several classes; "Light" prefixed marks are numerous (including photo software like "Lightroom", which I am explicitly not echoing). Moderate. The subtitle repeats "Swipe", which is also in competitor names, so rely on the keyword field for search.

### 4. Flickaway - "Swipe to keep or delete"
- **Rationale:** "Flick it away" is exactly the gesture. Descriptive and fun.
- **EN / ES:** EN: clear. ES: "flick" is not common in Spanish, readable but a bit opaque.
- **Availability:** No exact "Flickaway" found. **Near-collisions are heavy:** Flick: Swipe Photo Cleaner, Flick: Duplicate Photo Cleaner, Flicky (Camera Roll Cleaner), Flic, Flick Delete, Picflick, FlicPic, Flick Cleaner, plus a 2011 game "Flick Away". A user searching "flick" will see many look-alikes; confusion risk is high.
- **Trademark concerns:** Likely confusing similarity to existing photo-cleaner "Flick" marks in the same category. Moderate to high. Ranked 4 for this reason.

### 5. Lessy - "Swipe away photo clutter"
- **Rationale:** "Less" + friendly "-y": a phone with less stuff on it. Short and cute.
- **EN / ES:** EN: obvious "less". ES: "Lessy" is easy to read and say, "less" is understood by many; no negative meaning I know of (verify with a native reader).
- **Availability:** No exact "Lessy" found. Near: LessPhotos (photo storage cleaner, same job). Ending "-y" names are commonly used by small utilities.
- **Trademark concerns:** Unknown; coined word, possibly registrable, but near LessPhotos in function. Moderate. Ranked last because the name says less about the app than #1-#3.
