## Words to avoid in name, subtitle, keywords, description, screenshots

Hard no: Tinder (any form), Bumble, Hinge, competitor app names, "Apple", "iOS", "iPhone" inside the app name or keywords, "Photos" alone as the app name, "#1", "best", "award-winning" (unsubstantiated), "free up space instantly", "AI", "smart cleaner", "duplicate finder" (not a feature), "virus", "boost", "speed up your phone", "secure vault".
Use carefully: "free" (accurate only while there are no IAP or ads), "private" (accurate: nothing leaves the device; do not say "encrypted" or "secure" without basis), "delete" (say where it goes: Recently Deleted).
