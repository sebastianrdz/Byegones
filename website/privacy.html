Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-21
Developer: Sebastian Rodriguez
Contact: sebasstian.rdz@gmail.com

The short version

Byegones does not collect, store on our servers, transmit, sell or share any of your data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code. Your photos and videos stay on your iPhone.

What the app accesses, and why

  • Your Photos library (photos and videos). Byegones asks for permission to read and modify your Photos library. It uses this access only to show your items one at a time, display each item's date, size and duration, mark items as Favorite when you choose, and delete items you have chosen to delete. Deletions happen only after you confirm them in the standard iOS confirmation dialog.
  • Limited access. You can give the app access to only some of your photos. The app works with whatever you select. You can change this at any time in Settings.
  • File size. To show how much space an item uses, the app asks the system for the item's file size. This stays on your device.

Your photos, videos and their metadata are processed on your device only. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else by the app.

iCloud Photos. If an item is stored in iCloud and not on your phone, the app asks the Photos system to download it so you can view it. That transfer is performed by Apple's Photos and iCloud services between your device and your iCloud account. It is governed by Apple's terms and privacy policy, not by us, and we receive nothing.

What is stored on your device

The app keeps a small amount of data in its own storage on your iPhone:

  • A list of the identifiers of items you have already reviewed and items waiting in your trash pile. These are internal Photos identifiers, not the photos themselves.
  • A running total of the storage you have freed with the app.
  • A setting that remembers whether you have finished the introduction screens.

This data never leaves your device through the app. Like other app data, it may be included in your own device or iCloud backups, which Apple controls.

How to delete this data

  • Reset progress: in the app, go to Settings and choose Reset progress to clear the review history the app stores.
  • Uninstall: deleting the app from your iPhone removes all data the app stores.

Items you delete with the app move to the Recently Deleted album in Photos, where iOS keeps them for 30 days before removing them permanently. You can restore or permanently remove them from the Photos app.

Ratings prompt

The app may occasionally ask you to rate it using Apple's built-in rating prompt. Apple controls this prompt. We do not receive your answer or any personal information from it.

Third parties

Byegones contains no third-party SDKs, advertising, analytics or tracking. We do not sell or share personal information, and the app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Website

The Byegones website is a static site. It does not use analytics, cookies or trackers, and it loads no external fonts or scripts. Like most web hosts, the host (Vercel) may keep standard technical server logs, such as IP address and request time.

Children

Byegones is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the app collects no data, there is nothing to delete or disclose about any user.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold your personal data, there is no personal data held by us to access, correct, export or delete. You control all data in the app and in your Photos library directly. If you have a question about privacy, contact us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this policy and the effective date above before the change takes effect, and describe it in the app's release notes where appropriate.
