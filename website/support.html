Support

Quick answers below. Still stuck? Email us at sebasstian.rdz@gmail.com and we will help.

Is Byegones really free?

Yes. There are no in-app purchases, no subscriptions and no ads.

I deleted photos but my storage didn't go down. Why?

iOS keeps deleted photos and videos in the Recently Deleted album for 30 days, and the space is only fully freed when that album is emptied. To get it back right away, open Photos > Albums > Recently Deleted > Select > Delete All.

Can I undo a swipe?

Yes. Tap Undo to take back your last decision: keep, delete or favorite. Items in your trash pile can also be taken out any time before you delete them.

How do I reset my progress?

Open Settings (the gear on the main screen) > Reset progress > Reset. Photos you have already reviewed will show up again for review. Your trash pile and your total cleared are kept, and nothing is deleted from your library.

I only shared some photos. Can I change that?

Yes. When you share a limited selection, Byegones shows a banner with a Change selection button. You can also change it in iPhone Settings > Byegones > Photos.

What data do you collect?

None. There is no account, no tracking and no analytics, and your photos and videos stay on your iPhone. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Some photos take a moment to appear. What is going on?

Photos stored in iCloud need to be downloaded to your iPhone before they can show. Check your connection and give it a moment, then try again.

I tapped Cancel on the delete confirmation. Did anything get deleted?

No. If you cancel, nothing is deleted and your trash pile stays exactly as it was.
